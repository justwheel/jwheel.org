*Justin Wheeler* is a Free Software activist and Open Source community architect focused on the people behind digital infrastructure.
Justin currently works at link:/categories/red-hat/[Red Hat], where he serves as AI Alignment Community Architect for the RHEL Upstreams ecosystem in the link:https://redhatofficial.github.io/[Open Source & AI Program Office (OSAIPO)].
His work asks _“alignment with whom?”_
He guides communities through shaping the adoption of link:/tags/llm-gen-ai/[LLM-gen-AI] around their own values and interests, centering community values, contributor choice, and equitable access.
Justin's commitment to equitable access connects more than a decade of Fedora participation with earlier work at link:/categories/unicef/[UNICEF], where he mentored startups building link:/tags/digital-public-goods/[Digital Public Goods].
