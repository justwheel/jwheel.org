---

The term **LLM-gen-AI** originates from the [Software Freedom Conservancy](https://sfconservancy.org/llm-gen-ai/) (SFC).
In their *Recommendations When Using LLM-backed Generative AI Systems for FOSS Contributions*, the SFC introduced the shorthand to specifically describe systems that utilize Large Language Models to generate textual and code artifacts.
The term deliberately distinguishes these specific generative tools from broader concepts of artificial intelligence or machine learning, focusing the conversation on the legal, ethical, and community impacts of LLM-assisted contributions within software freedom.
